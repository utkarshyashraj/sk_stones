Frequently Asked Questions

Get clarity on our installation process, timelines, site requirements, quality checks, and execution standards.

What preparation is required before stone installation begins?

Site measurements, surface readiness, level checks, access planning, and material inspection are completed in advance to avoid execution issues and ensure smooth installation workflow.

How do you ensure accuracy during installation execution?

Accuracy is maintained through precise measurements, alignment checks, experienced supervision, proper tooling, and stage-wise verification throughout the installation process.

What factors affect installation timelines and scheduling?

Timelines depend on site readiness, material availability, design complexity, area size, weather conditions, and coordination with other ongoing site activities.

How is quality checked during and after installation?

Quality is verified through surface finish inspection, alignment consistency checks, joint uniformity assessment, stability testing, and final walkthrough before project handover.

Do you handle on-site challenges during execution?

Yes, our team addresses on-site challenges through immediate assessment, corrective planning, coordination adjustments, and practical solutions without compromising installation quality.
