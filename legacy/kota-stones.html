Product Overview

Our experienced stone specialists source Lime Stones based on density, structural strength, surface finish quality, and weather resistance — ensuring suitability for high-performance flooring and long-term outdoor exposure.

Our handling process emphasizes:

  • Accurate size grading 
  • Surface consistency control 
  • Shade grouping 
  • Structured sorting 
  • Reliable logistics management 

Ensuring Lime Stones retains its natural integrity and performs consistently across varied architectural applications.
